Development Timeline: 10–13 Days
Tech Stack: Streamlit + Gemini + Python + SQLite
Status: Planning Phase

---

# Vision

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered Career Operating System designed to help students become placement-ready through personalized analysis, intelligent recommendations, and measurable progress tracking.

Rather than acting as a chatbot, CareerPilot AI functions as an AI decision engine that continuously evaluates a student's career readiness and identifies the highest-impact actions needed to improve employability.

Our goal is to build a product that feels like an early-stage startup rather than a college assignment.

---

# Mission Statement

Empower every student with an AI system that removes uncertainty from career preparation by providing clear priorities, structured guidance, and continuous progress tracking.

---

# Problem Statement

Students preparing for placements face several challenges:

- They don't know what to learn first.
- They receive scattered advice from multiple sources.
- They cannot accurately evaluate their readiness.
- They struggle to prioritize their efforts.
- They lack personalized guidance.

Current AI tools solve individual problems such as resume reviews or interview preparation, but they do not provide a unified career strategy.

CareerPilot AI solves this by combining multiple career insights into a single intelligent platform.

# Product Philosophy

Every feature must satisfy three principles:

1. Solve a real user problem.
2. Produce measurable outcomes.
3. Contribute to the overall Career Score.

If a feature does not satisfy these principles, it will not be included in the MVP.

---

# Product Pillars

CareerPilot AI is built around three pillars.

## 1. Assess

Understand the student's current career readiness.

Examples:

- Resume Analysis
- Project Evaluation
- Career Score
- Skill Assessment

---

## 2. Plan

Generate personalized next steps.

Examples:

- Daily Missions
- Learning Roadmap
- Next Best Actions
- Priority Recommendations

---

## 3. Improve

Track measurable progress over time.

Examples:

- Career Score History
- Completed Tasks
- Progress Analytics
- Readiness Trends

---

# Core Differentiator

CareerPilot AI is not a collection of AI tools.

It is a unified Career Operating System where every module contributes to one continuously evolving Career Score.

Instead of asking users:

"What do you want to do?"

CareerPilot AI answers:

"What should you do next?"

# Career Score Engine

CareerPilot AI revolves around a single Career Score.

The score represents a student's overall placement readiness.

Initial scoring model

Resume ............... 25%

Projects ............. 25%

Interview ............ 20%

Skills ............... 15%

Roadmap Progress ..... 10%

Consistency .......... 5%

The scoring weights may evolve during development.

---

# AI Philosophy

Gemini is not the product.

Gemini is the intelligence layer powering the product.

The application should never feel like an AI chat interface.

Instead, AI should quietly analyze information and produce structured recommendations.

---

# AI Design Principles

Every AI response must:

- Return structured JSON.
- Be deterministic whenever possible.
- Provide actionable recommendations.
- Explain reasoning where appropriate.
- Never expose raw prompts to users.
